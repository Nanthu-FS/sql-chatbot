# Teyvat DMG Calc (Android)

An offline Android app that calculates Genshin Impact damage for a party of up to four characters from their
stats, weapons, artifacts and team buffs — plus a library of every character, weapon, artifact set and
elemental reaction.

**APK:** [`apk/genshin-damage-calc-debug.apk`](apk/genshin-damage-calc-debug.apk) (built by CI on every push; Android 8.0+).

## Features

- **Party calculator** – pick up to 4 characters; set level, constellation, talent levels, weapon + refinement,
  artifact sets (4pc or 2+2), main stats and substat totals — or type in the stats from the in-game attribute
  screen ("Character screen" mode).
- **Every damage row** of every talent (Normal/Charged/Plunge, Skill, Burst, plus damage from passives and
  constellations), each shown as non-crit / crit / average, with Vaporize/Melt/Aggravate/Spread variants and a
  per-hit breakdown (base, DMG bonus, CRIT, DEF and RES multipliers).
- **Reactions** – transformative (Overloaded, Swirl, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon…), Lunar-Charged/Bloom/Crystallize
  and Stellar-Conduct/Stellar Swirl, Crystallize shields.
- **Team buffs applied automatically** – all 127 character kits (passives + constellations), 245 weapon passives
  (every 3–5★ weapon), 50+ artifact sets, elemental resonance and the Moonsign mechanic. Conditional buffs are
  toggles/stack counters in the **Buffs** tab; anything else can be added as a custom buff.
- **Enemy** – level and per-element RES, or pick one of 347 enemies; RES shred and DEF reduction are applied.
- **Library** – searchable characters (talents, passives, constellations, base stats by level), weapons (stats by
  level, refinements), artifact sets and reaction guides with formulas.

## Accuracy

The engine follows the game's damage formula (cross-checked against Genshin Optimizer):

```
DMG = (Talent% × Stat × Multipliers + Flat DMG) × (1 + DMG Bonus) × CRIT × DEF × RES × Amplifying
DEF = (Lv + 100) / ((Lv + 100) + (EnemyLv + 100) × (1 − DEF reduction) × (1 − DEF ignore))
Amplifying = 1.5/2 × (1 + 2.78 × EM / (EM + 1400) + bonus)        Additive: +1.15/1.25 × LvMult × (1 + 5 × EM / (EM + 1200))
Transformative = LvMult × multiplier × (1 + 16 × EM / (EM + 2000) + bonus) × RES
Lunar / Stellar = Talent% × Stat × 3 / 1 / 1.6 × (1 + 6 × EM / (EM + 2000) + bonus) × (1 + base bonus) × CRIT × RES × (1 + elevation)
```

Unit tests (`core/src/test`) check base stats against in-game values, every formula, end-to-end hits computed by
hand (Hu Tao, Kazuha/VV swirls, party buffs, Lunar-Charged with Moonsign, Stellar-Conduct), and that every kit,
weapon and set references real damage rows and produces finite numbers.

## Data

`core/src/main/resources/gamedata/*.json` is generated by `tools/datagen/generate.py` from
[genshin-db](https://github.com/theBowja/genshin-db) (game version 7.1) and the stat curves of
[Genshin Optimizer](https://github.com/frzyc/genshin-optimizer) (both MIT). Hit categories/elements were checked
against Genshin Optimizer's character sheets; corrections and extra damage rows live in `tools/datagen/overrides.py`.

```
python3 tools/datagen/generate.py --cache /tmp/gdb-cache
```

## Build and test

- `core/` – pure Kotlin/JVM engine and data (no Android dependency): `./gradlew :core:test`
- `app/` – Jetpack Compose UI: `./gradlew :app:assembleDebug` (needs the Android SDK; the `:app` module is only
  included when `ANDROID_HOME`/`local.properties` points to one)

CI (`.github/workflows/genshin-calc-android.yml`) runs the unit tests, builds the APK, runs an emulator smoke test
that walks every screen and checks the displayed damage against the engine, then a 3,000-event monkey test.
The APK and the device report (screenshots, logs) are committed back to `apk/` and `device-report/`.

## Limitations

- Effects that depend on fight timing (stacks over time, buffs with partial uptime) are modelled as toggles or
  stack counters with sensible defaults — adjust them in the Buffs tab.
- Lunar reactions are shown per triggering character; the game's multi-character weighting (highest + ½ second +
  1/12 rest) is not combined across characters.
- Hexerei-specific upgrades and a few random/proc weapon effects (e.g. extra AoE hits) are not modelled.
